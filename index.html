      Model version 2026.6, updated July 2026. Federal brackets and the standard deduction use IRS tax-year 2026 figures. RMDs begin at the applicable age based on birth year, generally 73 or 75. Amounts use the IRS Uniform Lifetime Table. The 2026 Medicare income thresholds and Part B + Part D surcharges are modelled with the normal two-year MAGI lookback. Future thresholds and surcharges are indexed using your inflation assumption.

      Comparison basis. Equal take-home cost reduces the Roth or brokerage contribution by the value of the deduction given up, so every path costs the same today. Equal account contribution keeps the gross contribution the same and requires extra take-home pay; the pre-tax path keeps the deduction as spare take-home and invests it in its own taxable account, so all three strategies are real portfolios rather than one being adjusted by a hypothetical balance.

      India assumptions. Section 89A and Form 10EE address timing for specified foreign retirement accounts. They do not create a stated Roth exemption. The Roth menu therefore runs three assumptions. Treaty pension treatment, RNOR years, foreign-tax credits and Indian residency outcomes depend on facts the calculator cannot establish.

      Limits. This is one deterministic path. It does not model market sequence risk, AMT, NIIT, itemised deductions, tax-exempt interest, state residency after a move, expatriation tax, inherited-account tax, long-term care or plan-specific distribution rules. The temporary enhanced senior deduction is included only for calendar years 2026 through 2028. After-tax account values use a ten-year marginal drawdown estimate, not a one-year liquidation. Taxable-account dividend drag before withdrawal is not modelled. Early-withdrawal penalties and account-access rules before age 59½ are not modelled. India tax slabs and the entered exchange rate are held constant in today's terms.

      Methods and official sources
      • IRS 2026 tax adjustments
      • IRS Publication 590-B and Uniform Lifetime Table
      • CMS 2026 Medicare premiums and IRMAA
      • SSA IRMAA two-year lookback
      • India Section 89A
