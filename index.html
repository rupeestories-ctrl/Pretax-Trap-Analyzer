      Model version 2026.3, updated July 2026. Federal brackets and the standard deduction use IRS tax-year 2026 figures. RMDs use the IRS Uniform Lifetime Table. The 2026 Medicare income thresholds and Part B + Part D surcharges are modelled with the normal two-year MAGI lookback. Future thresholds and surcharges are indexed using your inflation assumption.

      India assumptions. Section 89A and Form 10EE address timing for specified foreign retirement accounts. They do not create a stated Roth exemption. The Roth menu therefore runs three assumptions. Treaty pension treatment, RNOR years, foreign-tax credits and Indian residency outcomes depend on facts the calculator cannot establish.

      Limits. This is one deterministic path. It does not model market sequence risk, AMT, NIIT, itemised deductions, tax-exempt interest, state residency after a move, expatriation tax, inherited-account tax, long-term care or plan-specific distribution rules. The temporary enhanced senior deduction is included only for calendar years 2026 through 2028. After-tax account values use a fixed ten-year marginal drawdown estimate, not a one-year liquidation. Modelled tax excludes tax on salary unrelated to this decision, but includes the extra working-year tax created by giving up the pre-tax deduction. When conversion tax is paid from brokerage, tax triggered by selling brokerage assets is not separately grossed up. Taxable-account dividend and capital-gain-distribution drag before withdrawal is not modelled. Early-withdrawal penalties and account-access rules before age 59½ are not modelled. India tax slabs and the entered exchange rate are held constant in today's terms.
