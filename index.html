04

What to do about it

Ordered by deadline, not by size.

    Assumptions and limits. Federal brackets and the standard deduction use IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32 figures for tax year 2026, indexed forward at your inflation rate. RMDs use the IRS Uniform Lifetime Table and begin at 73 for those born 1951–1959 and 75 for those born 1960 or later. Social Security taxation uses the standard provisional-income test. IRMAA tiers are estimates and lag actual income by two years. India uses the new-regime slabs with 4% cess and surcharge; the model applies a foreign tax credit against Indian liability where both countries tax the same dollar. Capital gains inside the brokerage are taxed at a flat long-term rate on the gain portion of each withdrawal. DTAA Article 20 relief on periodic pension payments is applied only when you model surrendering US status, because the treaty saving clause preserves US taxing rights over its own citizens and green card holders regardless of where they live.

    This is a projection, not advice. It is a single deterministic path, not a Monte Carlo. It ignores sequence-of-returns risk, long-term care, state estate tax, the temporary senior deduction, NIIT, and every fact specific to you. The India-side treatment of Roth accounts is genuinely unresolved — Section 89A and Form 10EE align the timing of Indian taxation with the US, but nothing in Indian law recognizes a Roth distribution as exempt. Model both outcomes; do not plan around one. Confirm everything with a cross-border CPA and a CA before you move money.
